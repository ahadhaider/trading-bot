## 🎬 MASTER REALISM STYLE GUIDE (हर scene पर apply करें — video-gen tool में global style prompt की तरह इस्तेमाल करें)

**STYLE (सभी scenes के लिए):**
Hyper-realistic cinematic live-action style, shot on ARRI/RED cinema camera look, 35mm lens depth-of-field, natural film grain, realistic skin texture with pores and imperfections, physically accurate lighting (soft key + rim light), true-to-life fabric and metal textures, natural muscle and cloth movement obeying gravity and momentum. यह ek real-world shot हुई film jaisa dikhna chahiye — cartoon, anime, 3D render, video-game cutscene ya "AI-generated" plastic look bilkul nahi.

**FIGHT / ACTION REALISM (sabhi combat scenes ke liye — spider, wolf, bear, dragon, tower guardian, tournament fights sab par apply):**
- Weight aur momentum physically accurate hone chahiye — koi bhi hit "floaty" ya weightless nahi lagna chahiye.
- Har impact par realistic reaction: dust kicking up, ground cracking, debris flying, muscles straining, breathing heavy, sweat/blood-spatter physics (bina graphic gore ke).
- Camera handheld-jaisa halka shake, motion blur on fast movement, quick realistic focus pulls — jaise ek asli film crew battle shoot kar raha ho.
- Beasts/dragons ke movement mein realistic animal biomechanics ho (fur/scale ripple, muscle flex under skin, correct weight-shift while lunging or landing) — na ki stiff/rigged 3D-model jaisa motion.
- Fire, energy blasts, aur magical effects photorealistic VFX jaise dikhne chahiye (real film composite jaisa), not glossy cartoon glow.
- Slow-motion sirf key impact moments par, baaki normal speed real-time combat feel ke saath.

**GLOBAL NEGATIVE (हर scene ke NEGATIVE field mein already jud gaya hai — video-gen tool में हर prompt ke end mein bhi manually paste kar sakte hain):**
cartoonish or anime style, 3D-render / video-game cutscene look, plastic/glossy CGI skin, floaty weightless movement, rigid stiff character animation, obvious AI-generated artifacts, warped or extra limbs, inconsistent character design between scenes, unnatural lighting, over-saturated glow effects, watermark, text overlay, low-resolution blur, uncanny-valley faces.
